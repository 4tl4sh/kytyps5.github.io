For **ASTRO BOT**, title ID **PPSA21567**, game versions **01.018.000** and **01.019.000**.

Combines renderer and delta-time options with physics and crash fixes, plus optional memory and video settings.
Includes separate packages for standard KytyPS5/U59 builds and the KytyPS5 DLSS fork.
The included README reports **1.018 tested**; the **1.019 ports have not been playtested**.

Extract the ZIP, choose the folder matching your game version and emulator, and copy its `PPSA21567.json` into `_Patches` beside the launcher. Restart the launcher and review **Cheats**.
